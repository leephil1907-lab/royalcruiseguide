BEFORE YOU SET SAIL

Website terms

Last updated: October 16, 2026

These general terms are a starting point for the Royal Cruise Travel Guide website. The operator should customize them with its correct legal identity, jurisdiction, contact details and any travel-seller disclosures required before soliciting bookings or payments.

Enquiries are not bookings

Submitting a form requests information only. It does not reserve a cabin, guarantee a fare, confirm availability or create a travel contract. A reservation is confirmed only through the applicable official booking process and subject to the supplier's terms.

Pricing and availability

This website does not claim to display verified live cruise fares or inventory. Fares may vary by sailing, cabin category, occupancy, taxes, port expenses, fees, promotions and time of purchase. Any current fare must be checked against a reliable supplier source and presented with its inclusions, exclusions and relevant conditions.

Independent travel guidance

Unless explicitly disclosed otherwise, this website is an independent cruise travel-guide concept and is not the official website of Royal Caribbean International or another cruise line. Cruise line names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Do not imply endorsement or affiliation without authorization.

Third-party suppliers

Cruise operators, airlines, hotels and excursion providers may impose their own terms. Before committing, confirm itinerary, cabin, travel documents, accessibility, insurance needs, cancellation rules, payment deadlines and all costs.

Website content

Destination descriptions and inspiration are general planning information, not guarantees of weather, port calls, facilities or future itineraries. Suppliers may change services and schedules.

Acceptable use and updates

Do not misuse the website or submit deceptive information. These terms may be updated as the service changes. Rights that cannot legally be excluded remain unaffected.

Operator and contact information

Before public launch, publish the actual operator identity, a verified contact method and any legally required travel-seller registration or disclosure details. This general template is not legal advice.
