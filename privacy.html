YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This starter notice explains intended handling of information submitted through this website. The operator must review and customize it to match actual practices and applicable US state and federal privacy requirements before launch.

Information you provide

A connected enquiry form may collect your name, email address, destination preference, travel timing, party size, budget range and optional trip notes. Do not submit payment card numbers, passport numbers or sensitive identification information through a general enquiry form.

How information is used

Enquiry details should be used to respond to the request, understand travel preferences and follow up about the requested trip. If the form is connected to a database, email provider, analytics or other services, disclose those providers and their role here before collecting real customer data.

Sharing and retention

The operator should limit access to enquiry records, share details only as needed to respond to the request and establish a retention schedule. Do not claim information is never shared or retained unless that is accurate.

Cookies and third-party content

This starter site loads third-party web fonts and travel imagery. If analytics, advertising, chat widgets or other tracking tools are added, update this notice and implement any required consent controls.

Your choices

Visitors may choose not to submit a form. Before launch, publish a verified business contact method for privacy requests and ensure that requests for access, correction or deletion can be handled as required by applicable law.

Children's information

This site is intended for adults planning travel. Do not provide children's personal details unless necessary and appropriate for a specific travel enquiry.

Updates and contact

Update this notice when the website's data practices change. Add the actual operator identity and verified contact details before public promotion. This template is not legal advice.
