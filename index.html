GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful
answers.

A little clarity now makes planning the fun part easier.

Are the cruise prices on this site live? +

This guide does not display unverified live fares. Cruise prices vary by sailing date, cabin category, taxes, fees and availability. Confirm current, itemized pricing with a reliable supplier before making a decision.

Can I request a specific cruise line or ship? +

Yes. Include your preferred cruise line or ship in your enquiry notes, along with your departure city and approximate dates. Recommendations depend on actual sailing options.

Does sending an enquiry book my cruise? +

No. The form starts a conversation only. A booking is confirmed only through the applicable official reservation process and subject to supplier terms.

What should I compare before choosing? +

Review the itinerary, cabin category, port locations, departure-port travel, included meals and drinks, gratuities, taxes, cancellation rules, accessibility and travel documents.
